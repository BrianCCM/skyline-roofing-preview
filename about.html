About Skyline

A local, family owned roofing company based in Cedar City, protecting Utah homes for over ten years.

The person who quotes
your roof runs the crew

Skyline Roofing Specialists is a family owned business run by Branson Balthrop out of Cedar City, protecting Utah homes for over ten years across Iron and Washington County.

That matters in one practical way. The person who comes out and looks at your roof is the person who writes the bid, runs the crew, and answers the phone when you call afterwards. Nothing gets handed off to a salesman and then to a stranger.

We are licensed, insured, and an Owens Corning Preferred Contractor, and we carry the rest of the major asphalt and metal lines so the material gets picked to fit the roof rather than the other way round.
