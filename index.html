Start with the inspection
  1. 01

    We get on your roof

    A free inspection with no obligation. We walk the roof, show you what we find, and bring shingle samples so you can compare your options side by side instead of guessing from a brochure.

  2. 02

    You get a detailed bid

    Itemized and in writing, with the material and the labor broken out, so you can put it next to any other quote and compare the same things.

  3. 03

    We deal with your insurance

    If it is storm damage, we inspect for it, document it, and work directly with your homeowners insurance carrier so the claim is not your job to chase.

  4. 04

    The crew works, then cleans up

    Most homes are done in one to three days. The crew works long hours, covers what needs covering, and leaves the site clean before they go.
