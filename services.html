Services

Roofing, and the exterior work that goes with it, for homes and businesses from Cedar City to St. George. Free estimates on every one of them.

Roofing

Residential and commercial. Asphalt and metal. Every job gets the same deck inspection, the same underlayment spec, and the same clean site at the end of it.

01

Asphalt shingles

Skyline has you covered with the best materials and manufacturers to choose from, including Owens Corning, Certainteed, GAF, Tamko, Pabco and Malarkey.

02

Storm restoration

Here in Southern Utah we get all sorts of storms, including wind and hail. When they hit, we thoroughly inspect for signs of damage and work directly with your homeowners insurance carrier to make the claim process easy for you.

03

Roof repairs

Roofs are not bulletproof, so repairs are much needed. Let us know the situation, whether it is a leaky roof, a wind damaged shingle, or anything at all you are concerned about.

04

Metal roofing

If you are looking to upgrade your home, metal roofing may be a good option. The two popular choices are exposed fastened panels and standing seam. Give us a call and we can discuss the right metal roof for you.

05

Yearly maintenance

Every year materials take a beating from the weather, and the biggest thing most people do not talk about is re-seal. There may be exposed nails or old caulking on your roof you do not know about. We make sure they are buttoned up and ready for the next storm.

06

Emergency tarping

Cedar City and the surrounding towns get crazy wind every year, and it can cause significant damage. We will come out within 24 hours to make sure your roof is properly sealed temporarily, until we have the material to repair it.

07

New construction

Any project, big or small. We would love to take on more work and offer free estimates to contractors and homeowners who request it. Let us know if you have a new project in mind and we will get started.

08

Tile lift & resets

Tile roofs are popular in the warmer areas, and St. George falls under that category. We offer a complete underlayment tear out and replace while lifting and resetting the existing tiles.

The rest of the exterior

The roof is only half of how water leaves your house. We handle the other half too, so you are not coordinating two contractors on one job.

09

Gutters

Gutter installation, replacement and repair, handled alongside the roof so the whole water path works together.

10

Soffit & fascia

Repair and replacement of the soffit and fascia that close in your eaves and carry your gutters.

11

Siding

Siding installation and repair, available with roofing work or on its own.
