Storm damage & insurance claims

Wind and hail are what put most Southern Utah roofs into a claim. If that is what happened to yours, the inspection is free and we deal with your carrier directly.

Do you actually
have damage?

Worth knowing before you call anyone. Wind damage you can often spot from the ground. Hail damage you usually cannot, which is why so much of it goes unclaimed until the roof starts leaking years later.

Signs of wind damage

  • Shingles missing, lifted or creased along a horizontal line
  • Ridge cap shingles cracked or gone
  • Granules collecting at the bottom of downspouts
  • Flashing pulled away from walls, chimneys or vents
  • Debris on the roof or in the yard after a storm

Signs of hail damage

  • Dark bruises that feel soft when pressed, like a dented apple
  • Dents in soft metal: vents, flashing, gutters, the AC condenser
  • Granule loss exposing the black mat underneath
  • Random strike pattern, rather than the uniform wear of age
  • Spatter marks on decks, fences or painted surfaces

Most policies have a window for filing after a storm, so if you think something happened, it is worth getting it looked at rather than waiting to see if it leaks.

The questions
everybody asks

Do I have to pay anything?

Your deductible. That is the part of the repair your policy makes you responsible for, and it is the same whether you use us or anyone else. Be careful with any contractor who offers to cover it or make it disappear. That is insurance fraud, and it puts you on the hook, not just them.

What does insurance actually pay for?

Most policies pay either actual cash value or replacement cost. Actual cash value subtracts depreciation for the age of your roof, so you get less on an older roof. Replacement cost pays the depreciation back once the work is finished. Your declarations page says which one you have, and it is worth knowing before the adjuster arrives.

Will my premium go up if I file?

It depends on your carrier, your claim history, and whether the storm was widespread. We are roofers, not agents, so the honest answer is to ask yours before you file. What we can tell you is whether you actually have damage, which is the part that should drive the decision.

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. Free estimates and inspections, claim or no claim. If we get up there and your roof is fine, that is the answer you get.

How long does the whole thing take?

The claim side is mostly your carrier's pace. The roof itself is usually one to three days once material is on site.

My roof is leaking right now. What do I do?

Call us. We will come out within 24 hours to make sure the roof is properly sealed temporarily, until we have the material to repair it properly. Do not wait on the claim to stop water coming in.
